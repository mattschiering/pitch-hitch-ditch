Pitch, Hitch, or Ditch

Market the right product to the right customer at the right stage of the buyer journey.

How to play

  1. Every team draws a Buyer Persona, a Product and a Buyer Journey card.
  2. All teams prepare their pitch at the same time. You get 90 seconds.
  3. Then each team pitches live to the judges, in front of the room.
  4. Pass or fail, you draw three new cards next round.
  5. Teams keep every Journey stage they pass and never draw it again. Collect all four to finish!
